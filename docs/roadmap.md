## Entertainment sprints (incremental release track)

- Sprint 1: Bot match completion, rematch and tournament replay — deployed.
- Sprint 2: Local player profile, XP, daily quests and match history — deployed.
- Sprint 3: Invite-link PvP, reconnect UI and two-player rematch — frontend deployed; live two-device acceptance pending.
- Sprint 4: Public guest matchmaking by H — frontend deployed, backend CI passed; waiting-room cancellation, stale-room cleanup and live acceptance pending.
- Sprint 5: Cosmetic-only garden themes unlocked at 0/100/300 XP; local browser persistence. No ads, payments, purchases or advantage in payoffs. Release requires frontend CI/deployment and manual visual check.

Monetization gate: do not add paid cosmetics or rewarded ads until privacy-respecting, consent-aware retention and completion metrics have been implemented and evaluated. XP is browser-local and is not a secure entitlement or purchase ledger.
